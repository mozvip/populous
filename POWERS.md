# Populous (1989) - Divine Powers Guide

In the original Populous (released in 1989), divine powers are called "divine interventions" or "acts of God". Unlike more recent episodes such as Populous: The Beginning (1998), the number of spells is more limited and focuses mainly on terrain manipulation.

Here is the list of available interventions:

## 1. Terrain Sculpting (Elevation / Lowering)

This is not a "spell" in the strict sense since it's the basic action, but it consumes mana. It allows you to create flat areas for your followers to build larger structures (castles), or to submerge enemy lands to drown their populations.

## 2. Earthquake

Shakes a precise area, causing fissures and chaotically altering the terrain's altitude. It's an excellent way to destroy the foundations of enemy cities and turn their castles into simple tents.

## 3. Swamp

Creates a stagnant water zone on a flat terrain tile. Any character (ally or enemy) who walks on it is instantly engulfed and dies. It's a very effective defensive or harassment spell.

## 4. Knight

Transforms your current leader (the bearer of the Ankh) into an extremely powerful hero. The Knight roams the map autonomously to burn enemy houses and kill their inhabitants. Each destroyed building increases your mana.

## 5. Volcano

Causes a mountain to erupt in the middle of an area, covering the surroundings with lava and impassable rocks. This destroys homes and makes the terrain unbuilidable for a long time, forcing your opponent to do massive earthworks.

## 6. Flood

One of the most devastating spells. It raises the general ocean level by one notch. All inhabitants located on low-lying lands (at sea level) are immediately drowned, which can wipe an entire civilization off the map if they haven't built at higher elevations.

## 7. Armageddon

The ultimate spell. Once cast, all structures in the world disappear and all followers (yours and your opponent's) converge toward the center of the map for a final fight to the death. The god whose followers win this battle wins the game.

## Note on the Ankh (Papal Magnet)

Although it is not an offensive spell, moving this object is crucial as it defines the rallying point for your followers and where your next Knight will be born.
